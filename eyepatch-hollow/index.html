02Content guideline
Step1

Point at the spot

Don't read these word for word. Say it your way.
Directions
  • Bare face. Press your finger into the dip under the inner corner of your eye.
  • Flash close-ups of it in an inset. The more examples you show, the stronger the hook.
  • Don't do insets? Shoot it full frame, right up close on your eye.
Say something like
  • "okay these aren't dark circles"
  • "had these since I was 18. so like 7 years now"Your own age and years
Step2

Give it the right name

Don't read these word for word. Say it your way.
Directions
  • Stay on your face, finger still on the spot.
  • Put hollow under-eyes on screen as text.
  • Want a diagram of the eye area? Cut to it for one beat.
Say something like
  • "it's called under-eye hollowness"
  • "so concealer and brightening stuff never fixed it"Talk about product types. Never a brand
Step3

Scoop and stick

Don't read these word for word. Say it your way.
Directions
  • Scoop a patch out with the spatula. Get close on your hand.
  • Put it right on the dip you pointed at.
Say something like
  • "you scoop it so it doesn't tear"
  • "right on the hollow part"
Step4

The 30 minutes

Don't read these word for word. Say it your way.
Directions
  • Film the patch going from milky to clear. Stay quiet for that part.
  • Talk to the camera the rest of the time. A timer on screen works too.
  • Show it up close from the side.
Say something like
  • "30 minutes. that's it"
  • "okay it went clear"
Step5

Peel and land it on skin

Don't read these word for word. Say it your way.
Directions
  • Peel one off.
  • Last frame is your skin, the same spot you pointed at first.
Say something like
  • "the shadow looks softer right here"

Do's & Don'ts
Do
  • Film the close-ups bare-faced. No filter, no beauty mode
  • Keep the same spot in every skin shot
  • Say it looks plumper or the shadow looks softer
  • Reply to comments for the first 48 hours
Don't
  • No unboxing. Skip the PR box shots
  • Don't open on the jar
  • Don't say it fills the hollow or replaces filler
  • Don't say another brand doesn't work
  • Don't tag TikTok Shop products on this campaign
