Do's & Don'ts
Do
  • Cast 35+ with eye wrinkles or bags you can actually see
  • Film in one take, same angle and same light on both sides
  • Show the real size of the tube and an honest timeline
  • Say it as your experience, like the eye routine I tried before booking Botox
  • Spanish content? Add a 40+ caption and the coupon or free shipping
  • Check the orange cart is linked right after you upload
Don't
  • No wrinkle cream on someone with no wrinkles. People comment you don't have wrinkles
  • Don't squeeze cream into forehead creases to make wrinkles on purpose
  • No different light or makeup between the two sides. No cuts, no filter
  • No hooks that mock how someone looks
  • Don't make the tube or the results look bigger than they are
  • Don't say it replaces filler or Botox, or makes you 20 years younger
  • Don't rub it in with your fingers. Let the silver tip do it
  • Don't name other brands

Must-haves
